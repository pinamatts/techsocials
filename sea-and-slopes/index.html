Native charm, modern comfort.

Bamboo, nipa and wood on the outside. Hot showers, air-conditioning and a full kitchen inside. Sea in front, the hills of northern Cebu behind.

The Kamalig

The Kamalig sleeps fifteen.

Two big rooms under a native A-frame, each with its own bath, a loft, a refrigerator and a coffee set. Book it and the whole resort closes to everyone else.

  • Kamalig 1: a double bed, a bunk bed and a floor mattress
  • Kamalig 2: three bunk beds
  • Hot water in every bathroom
Mabini Hall

Mabini Hall is where everyone ends up.

The open hall on the ground floor holds the fully equipped kitchen, the long dining tables, a sitting area with a day bed, and the karaoke.

  • Cook your own food, the kitchen is ready
  • Free karaoke, and YouTube on the screen
  • A standby generator if the power goes out
Pool and beach

A pool steps from the sea.

Swim in the pool or walk straight onto the beach. Mornings, the sun comes up over the water right in front of you.

  • Private pool for your group only
  • Beachfront access and a BBQ area
  • A caretaker on the property the whole stay
The lawn

Room for a tent, a stage or a garden reception.

The manicured lawn takes glamping tents, team building games or a full garden reception. Camping tents and event tents can be set up for a fee.

Bring more people. Add a room.

The Kamalig takes 15. Each add-on room opens the resort to more of your group, up to 32 guests in all.
