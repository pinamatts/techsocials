Questions.

How do I get my ticket?

Right after you pay, your e-ticket with a QR code appears on screen and is sent to your email and mobile number. Show it at the gate, on your phone.

Can I give my ticket to someone else?

Yes. Forward the e-ticket. Each QR code lets one person in, once, so keep it private until the show.

I lost my e-ticket. What now?

Message us with your reference number or the email you used, and we'll send it again. The old QR code stops working when a new one is issued.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are refundable only if the show is cancelled or moved and you can't make the new date. (Sample policy, to be confirmed by Cebu Coliseum.)

Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome. Children aged 3 and up need their own ticket. (Sample policy, to be confirmed.)
