Questions

Good to know.

How do I get my ticket?

Right after you pay, your tickets open on screen, one per seat, and a link is sent to your email and mobile number. Show it at the gate on your phone.

Can I send a ticket to someone in my group?

Yes. Each seat has its own ticket and link, so everyone can walk in on their own phone. Each code lets one person in, once.

Why can't I just screenshot my ticket?

The QR code changes every 20 seconds, so a screenshot or a copied photo won't scan. It keeps your seat safe from fakes and scalpers.

I lost my ticket. What now?

Message us with your order number or the email you used, and we'll send it again. The old link stops working the moment a new one is issued.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are refunded in full if the show is cancelled, or if it's moved and you can't make the new date.
