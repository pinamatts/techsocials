    How far ahead should we book?

    Graduation season and December fill first. For those dates, send your inquiry two to three months ahead. For other dates, a few weeks is usually enough.

    Is there parking?

    There is no parking inside the Coliseum. We'll point you to the nearest pay parking when we confirm your date, and for big events we can help plan drop-off.

    Can we bring our own sound, lights and caterer?

    Yes, tell us in your inquiry and we'll include the ingress schedule and house rules with your quote.

    How do we pay?

    You get a written quote first. A reservation fee holds your date, and the balance is settled before the event. An official receipt is issued for every payment.
