Induction season

Planning your chapter's induction?

We are the stage behind JCI Cebu's 79th Induction, the first induction ceremony of the season in Central Visayas. Inductions run on a set program, and we plan the stage around it.

The solemn parts, done right

The anthem and the oath-taking get the flag and the oath on the wall, house lights down, and no ads on screen.

Your partners, seen all night

A partner logo loop during dinner and breaks, so every sponsor gets the screen time they paid for.

The turnover on the big screen

Live camera on the side screens for the gavel, the speeches and the photos.

How it works

From plan to show night.

  1. Plan it here

    Use the stage planner and send us your plan with your date and venue.

  2. Site visit

    We check the venue's power, ceiling height and load-in, then confirm the quote.

  3. Setup and rehearsal

    Our crew builds the stage and runs your videos and cues with your host.

  4. Show night

    A technician stays at the controls from doors open to the last song.
