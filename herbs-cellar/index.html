From Ormoc to Cebu

It started behind a hobbit‑hole door in Ormoc.

Before SM City Cebu, there was Ormoc Herbs: a garden restaurant you find by its little round door, set in lush green. Chef Jin Ho Yoo built four branches across Leyte on one idea: cook with the freshest herbs and local produce, and treat every recipe with respect for where it came from.

Herbs & Cellar is that idea, brought to Cebu with a wine cellar added. It opened on December 4, 2025, the first branch outside Leyte.
